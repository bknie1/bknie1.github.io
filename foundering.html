Foundering

A roguelite escape from a sinking ocean liner. Start deep in the flooding hull and climb your way up and out — before the water, the pressure behind the watertight doors, and the ship itself finish you.

You play a third-class passenger aboard a foundering liner. Every ship is procedurally generated from a seed and verified solvable, then assembled from real areas of the ship — boiler rooms and steerage down low, second class in the middle, the grand staircase and boat deck up top. Flee upward, seal hatches behind you, and reach the boats.

What's in it

  • A real flooding simulation — water flows through open hatches, backs up deck to deck, and builds pressure behind sealed doors until they burst.
  • Watertight doors & hold-breath swimming — seal the way behind you, or take a breath and swim a flooded compartment for the key.
  • Emergency pumps & power — restore power, run the pumps, buy time.
  • Seeded, always-solvable levels — a graph check guarantees a route out.
  • Passenger rescues & an Educational mode — save who you can and learn the real history of each area as you pass through it.
